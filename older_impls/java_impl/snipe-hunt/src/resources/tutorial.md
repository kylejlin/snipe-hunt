Huh?
What does "CannotDropRetreaterOnEnemysBackTwoRanks" mean?

To understand this error message, you must first understand what a _retreater_ is.
Notice how up to this point, all the animal steps we performed involved moving a card _forward_.
Most cards can only move forward.
However, certain cards, called _retreaters_, are also allowed to move backward.
You can tell if a card is retreater by counting the bright trapezoids at the top and bottom edges of the card.
The trapezoids indicate which direction cards can move, so if a card has two trapezoids (e.g., a [beta boar]), it can move both directions, but if it only has one (e.g., an [beta ox]), it can only move in that direction.

You are not allowed to drop retreaters on the back two ranks, but you can drop them on any other rank.
Non-retreaters can be dropped on any rank.

To recap:

The goal of the game is to capture your opponent's snipe.

Each turn, you can either (1) perform two animal steps, (2) perform a drop, or (3) perform a snipe step.

Cards are captured by activating triplets.
Triplets are activated by stepping onto a rank with an animal so that there are cards with 1, 2, and 3 dots of a given element.

Captured cards go to the reserve. They can be dropped on any rank, unless they are a retreater, in which case they cannot be dropped on the opponent's back 2 ranks.

Animals can only step one rank forward, unless they are a retreater, in which case they can step one rank in either direction.

There are some additional rules, which we'll discuss in the next pages.

[await click]

---

Animal step restrictions:

You cannot perform a step that empties a rank (step out and leave zero cards in it) unless that step captures the enemy snipe.

You cannot perform a step that captures your own snipe unless that step also captures the enemy snipe.

If you choose to spend your turn performing an animal step, you _must_ perform another animal step unless the first step captures the enemy snipe.
The second step is not optional.

You cannot perform a step with the same card twice on the same turn.
However, if you have duplicate animals (e.g., two dragons), those count as different cards.

[await click]

---

Snipe step restrictions:

You cannot perform a snipe step that empties a rank.

[await click]

---

Drop restrictions:

We already covered them all, so feel free to skip this page.
In case you forgot:

You need at least two cards in your reserve to make a drop.

Retreaters cannot be dropped on your opponent's back two rows.

[await click]

---

Miscellaneous rules:

If a player has no legal actions on their turn (i.e., they can't perform an animal step, drop, or snipe step), they lose the game.

Sometimes a rank already has a triplet on it.
In these situations, stepping into the rank with any animal that has at least one element dot of the same element as the triplet will activate the triplet.
If the entering animal does not have element dots of the same element as the triplet, that triplet is _not_ activated.
